On a serene morning, as the quiet rhythm of meditation settled and the breath deepened, a profound principle of existence unfolded. Change is the only constant in the physical world. The body, the mind, and the outer environment are in perpetual motion, shifting constantly, while the true Self remains untouched, eternal, and unchanging.

In this physical plane, uncertainty reigns. Nothing remains fixed or predictable. People change, fortunes rise and fall, and circumstances shift without warning. Resistance to these natural shifts only creates inner friction and suffering. True peace begins when one learns to fully accept the uncertain nature of the physical realm and anchors firmly within the stillness of the inner Self.

To navigate this changing world, one must cultivate continuous awareness—*eruka*. True awareness means being completely immersed in the present moment, whether walking, eating, or performing daily tasks. Great masters through the ages have shown that when action is infused with deep alertness, mechanical habits and inner conflicts naturally dissolve.

“ఎరుక ఉన్నప్పుడు దొంగతనం అనేది డిసపియర్ అయిపోతుంది.”

This spirit of complete dedication and pure awareness finds its highest expression in selfless service, mirroring the unconditional devotion of Hanuman, who embraced every task assigned by Sri Rama with profound joy, seeing no distinction between large or small duties.

The teaching closed with a clear sankalpa to practice mindful awareness in every moment, honoring the changing nature of the world while resting quietly in the unchanging light of the inner soul.
